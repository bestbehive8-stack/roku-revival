# 📺 Roku Revival

### Lost apps. Classic experiences. Rebuilt for today.

Welcome to **Roku Revival** — a community-driven project dedicated to preserving, recreating, and reimagining the classic Roku experiences that disappeared over the years.

Remember the old streaming apps, menus, designs, and channels that used to make Roku feel different?

We're bringing that feeling back.

## 🚀 REVIVE DELUXE

**REVIVE DELUXE** is the flagship project of Roku Revival.

It's a modern Roku experience inspired by the classic streaming era, combining:

- 🎬 Classic Roku-style interfaces
- 📺 Retro streaming experiences
- 🎵 Music and entertainment sections
- 🔴 Live-content support
- ⭐ Classic-inspired layouts
- 🔎 Modern search and navigation
- 🎮 Simple Roku remote controls
- 🛠️ Community-developed features

Our goal isn't to simply copy the past.

**We're rebuilding the experience for today.**

## 🕰️ Preserving Roku History

Many classic streaming apps and experiences have disappeared, changed, or become unavailable on modern devices.

Roku Revival is an attempt to document and recreate the **feeling and functionality** of that era through original software and legally authorized content.

## 🌟 Our Mission

> **Bring back the experiences people remember — while building something new.**

Roku Revival is an independent fan/community project and is **not affiliated with Roku or any television network, studio, or streaming service mentioned in this project.**

We do not distribute proprietary source code or unauthorized copyrighted media.

## 🧑‍💻 Built By The Community

Roku Revival is an open project.

Developers, designers, preservationists, Roku fans, and retro-media enthusiasts can contribute ideas, improvements, documentation, and original code.
